Welcome to Clueless

A session assistant that runs entirely on this Mac. It listens, transcribes, and answers questions on a hotkey.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Transcription, answers and summaries run on-device. Transcripts stay in memory and are never written to disk.

Invisible to screen share

The overlay is excluded from screen recording, so others on the call do not see it.

Recording needs consent

Laws on recording conversations vary by jurisdiction, and many require everyone's agreement. Tell participants when you use a transcription assistant, and follow your institution's policy.

Pick a persona to set tailored prompts, context sources and layout. Changeable any time in Settings.
