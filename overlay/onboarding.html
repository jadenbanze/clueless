Welcome to Clueless

A meeting assistant that runs entirely on this Mac. It listens, transcribes, and answers questions about the conversation on a hotkey.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Transcription, answers and summaries run on-device. Transcripts are kept in memory and never written to disk.

Invisible to screen share

The overlay is excluded from screen recording, so others on the call do not see it.

Recording needs consent

Laws on recording conversations vary by place, and many require everyone's agreement. Tell the people you meet that you use a transcription assistant, and follow your organisation's policy.

How will you use it?

This sets sensible defaults. You can change it any time in Settings.
